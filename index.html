Cataract · मोतियाबिंद

Cataract surgery, explained simply.

Cloudy or dim vision is common with age. Here is how a cataract visit usually works at Super Eye Hospital, with patient wards available for your stay.

Ask about cataract surgery

  1. Eye check-up

    A full examination to confirm the cataract and check overall eye health.

  2. Tests & planning

    Measurements for your lens and a clear explanation of the procedure and cost.

  3. Surgery

    Performed by the hospital's doctors, with clean wards for patients who stay.

  4. Follow-up & glasses

    Check-ups after surgery, and any new glasses from the in-house optical.
